Privacy Policy

This policy explains what QuickKit processes on your device and how its advertising and consent services work.

Effective and last updated: October 10, 2026

1. Overview

QuickKit is an Android toolkit for image, PDF, document, and QR tasks. Its core file-processing tools process selected content locally on your device where applicable. QuickKit does not need to upload your selected documents or images to QuickKit-operated servers for these tools.

Internet connectivity may be used for advertising, consent and privacy services, and actions you choose through Android or other external apps.

2. Information QuickKit processes

Depending on the features you use, QuickKit may process files and content you select or create, camera frames while using scanning features, and app preferences such as favorites and recent items. Word editing may keep local draft or recovery data so you can continue work after an interruption.

QuickKit does not operate an account or login service.

3. Files and local processing

You choose files using Android's supported file and photo selection interfaces. QuickKit may create temporary cache files while a task is running, local editing or recovery data, and generated output files. Outputs you save or export are yours and are not silently removed as temporary cache.

Core file processing happens locally on your device. A file may leave the device only when you choose an external action such as sharing it with another app or service.

4. Device permissions

Camera

Camera access is optional and used for QR scanning and Scan to PDF. Camera frames are processed for the feature you select. QuickKit does not upload camera frames to QuickKit servers.

Network

Network access supports Google advertising and consent services. Android may also use network connectivity when you choose an external system action.

Files are selected through Android's system pickers; QuickKit does not request broad shared-storage access for that purpose.

5. Advertising

QuickKit uses Google AdMob for interstitial and optional rewarded advertising. Interstitial ads may appear at eligible natural breaks after successful tool use. Core tools and actions such as saving, exporting, downloading, sharing, and copying are not gated behind an ad.

Google Mobile Ads may process advertising-related information, which can include advertising identifiers, device and app information, IP/network information, and ad interaction information, subject to Google's services, your settings, and applicable consent requirements.

6. Rewarded Ad-Free Pass

You may choose to watch a rewarded ad to receive a one-hour Ad-Free Pass. Participation is optional. The pass does not unlock core functionality and is not a subscription or purchase.

7. Consent and privacy choices

QuickKit uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and present consent choices where required. The choices available can depend on your region, app configuration, applicable law, and Google's consent platform. You can revisit privacy choices in the app when that option is available.

8. Third-party services

Google AdMob and Google UMP provide advertising and consent features. Google processes information under its own terms and policies. Review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information for advertising
  • Google AdMob

QuickKit does not include Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or a separate third-party analytics or tracking SDK in the inspected project configuration.

9. Local storage and retention

QuickKit-controlled preferences, favorites, recent-item information, temporary files, and editing or recovery data are stored locally as needed. Temporary data may be removed by QuickKit after use or through available Files & Storage controls. Local app data may also be removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Saved outputs remain where you chose to save them.

Google controls retention of information processed by its advertising and consent services. See Google's policies for details; QuickKit does not set a retention period for that data.

10. Data security

Local files and app data are handled using Android app storage and system file-selection mechanisms. No method of storage or transmission can be described as completely risk-free. When you share a file or follow an external link, the receiving service's practices apply.

11. Your choices

You can choose whether to use optional features, grant camera access, watch a rewarded ad, or share files through another app. You can manage Android permissions in system settings and clear QuickKit's local app data through Android settings. Consent options may be available in QuickKit where required.

12. Policy updates

This policy may be updated when QuickKit's features or practices change. The effective date above shows when this version was last updated. Please review this page periodically.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact the QuickKit project through the Ashish-Vision GitHub profile.
